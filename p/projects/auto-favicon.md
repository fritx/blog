# ✈️ auto-favicon: Your Title-to-Favicon Solution!

English | [简体中文](./auto-favicon-zh.md)

> Automatic Favicon Generation Based on Your Site's Title!

Tired of manually crafting favicons? auto-favicon is a lightweight JavaScript library that leverages HTML5 Canvas to generate a unique favicon from any text title. The standout feature: it automatically detects and utilizes the first emoji (if present) in your title, or elegantly selects the initial character as the basis for your icon.

**💖 Key Features**:

- **One-click solution** - Integrate with a single line of code and instantly give your site a personalized favicon.
- **Emoji compatibility** - Full support for emojis adds a touch of personality to your branding.
- **Smart character extraction** - Automatically analyzes titles to pick the most representative element.
- **Responsive design** - Ensures clarity and recognition across all screen sizes.

No more favicon headaches! We handle the details so you can focus on building great products. Try auto-favicon today and enjoy the convenience of seamless integration!

**🌈 Slogan**: Sets favicon for you based on title, with emoji support.
